Demonstration · not for clinical use

A clinical time-series case for the AION harness.

A real 12-lead ECG cohort plus a synthetic 48h ICU sepsis trajectory. Four incompatible input formats, one 2h / 4h / 6h early-warning objective, asymmetric clinical cost.

01

Inputs

What the agent reads.
12-lead ECG recordings at 250 Hz — real PTB cohort
48 hours of ICU vitals at 1-min resolution — synthetic sepsis
Legacy EDF file in a different gain & lead ordering
Cardiologist beat annotations for 8 specific beats
02

Expected outputs

What "done" looks like.
Per-patient MI prediction localising to the inferior-lateral leads
Per-minute sepsis probability at 2h / 4h / 6h horizons, well-calibrated
Four diagnostic plots consumed inside the final report
Sealed report with disclaimer, provenance, and failure-mode analysis
03

Challenges

What will trip the agent up.
Tiny cohort — 2 MI vs 1 healthy ECG, one synthetic ICU patient
Synthetic vs real provenance — must travel with the data, not be hidden
Sparse lactate — 16 measurements across 48 hours, irregular sampling
Non-stationary regimes — stable → onset → shock → resuscitation
Asymmetric clinical cost — a missed MI is far worse than a false alarm
Legacy EDF gain mismatch — silent nonsense if the adapter is sloppy
